A make.fun bounty entry: one satirical sentence, at most 320 characters,
roasting lawmakers who would regulate AI, crypto or the internet but cannot
convert a file to PDF.

## The entry

> They write the laws for AI, crypto, and the entire internet, yet their own tech bill reached the floor as a tilted phone photo of a Word file, glare and all, because the 'Save as PDF' button has survived three full terms in office without ever being discovered.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the sentence above to `/output/entry.txt` (UTF-8, no trailing
newline), exactly as the contest's gate expects: a single sentence of 1 to
320 characters.
